Yes, Studio Pro is completely free and 100% ad-free. There are no premium paywalls, no subscriptions, no credit card requirements, and no account creation required. You can convert, merge, compress, and edit unlimited documents.

Traditional tools upload your files to remote cloud servers for conversion. Studio Pro utilizes WebAssembly, PDF-Lib, and client-side JavaScript engines to perform all operations directly inside your device's browser memory. Your files never leave your computer or phone, ensuring strict compliance with FERPA, GDPR, and data protection privacy standards.
